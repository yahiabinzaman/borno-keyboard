Why Creators Choose Borno

Engineered from the ground up for Apple Silicon with uncompromised precision.

Zero-Preview Direct Typing (Windows Experience)

No annoying floating candidate boxes blocking your artboard or following your cursor. When you type phonetically, Borno converts seamlessly right in your text field just like Avro on Windows.

Apple Silicon Native

Pure ARM64 code built for M1, M2, M3, M4, and M5 chips. No Rosetta translation, instant responsiveness, and zero battery drain.

Adobe Illustrator & Design Ready

Flawlessly compatible with Bornomala, Kalpurush, and all Unicode Bengali fonts in Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, and InDesign.

100% Offline & Private by Design

Borno operates entirely on your Mac. No analytics, no cloud connections, no keystroke tracking. Your thoughts and designs remain strictly private.

Up & Running in 3 Easy Steps

Setup takes less than 60 seconds.

1

Download & Install

Download the official Borno DMG or install via Homebrew with a single command.

2

Add to Input Sources

Open System Settings → Keyboard → Input Sources, click +, search Borno and add it.

3

Switch & Type

Press ⌃ Control + ␣ Space (or the 🌐 Globe key) anytime to switch to Borno and write Bangla!
